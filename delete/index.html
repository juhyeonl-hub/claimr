App: Bootprint · Developer: an independent developer based in Finland

How to request deletion

Email us and we will delete your Bootprint account and all data associated with it:

Email juhyeonl.dev@gmail.com

Please send the request from the Google account email you linked, or include your in-app player name (e.g. runner-xxxx, shown on your Profile) so we can find your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and account identifier
  • Chosen teams and battles you joined or created
  • Map tiles you claimed
  • All session records (distance, duration, verification results)
  • Your email address and Google account link, if you linked one

Timing & data retention

  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Raw GPS traces are already deleted immediately after each session is verified. If a session is on hold, its raw points are kept at most 7 days; a coarse route summary is kept 14 days and a salted hash of your IP up to 30 days — solely to prevent abuse — then deleted automatically.
  • After account deletion, all remaining data is removed.

Prefer to just clear this device?

You can clear the app's local data anytime in the app: Profile → Reset data. To remove your data from our servers, use the email request above.
